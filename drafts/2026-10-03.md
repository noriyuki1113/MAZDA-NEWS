### Mazda and ENEOS Team Up to Promote E20 Bioethanol Fuel

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a collaboration to raise awareness and accelerate adoption of low-carbon fuels. At the 37th Media Endurance Roadster (MX-5) Race, scheduled for October 3–4, 2026 at Fuji Speedway, approximately 20 Roadster race cars will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE E20, a gasoline blend containing 20% plant-derived bioethanol. The partnership aims to let media participants directly experience the performance of low-carbon fuel, helping to build broader public understanding of sustainable energy options.

**Why it matters:** As Mazda actively pursues carbon-neutral solutions beyond electrification, this real-world motorsport trial of E20 bioethanol signals the brand's commitment to diversified, drop-in fuel alternatives that could eventually benefit internal-combustion Mazda owners worldwide.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Mark 787B's 35th Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B racing car. The collectible is available in two sizes — a palm-sized 100% (~7 cm) and a larger 400% (~28 cm) — both featuring the 787B's distinctive livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th-anniversary message on the reverse. The release marks 35 years since the 787B became the first Japanese car to win overall at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1991.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda enthusiasts, this is a rare officially licensed piece of 787B memorabilia that celebrates one of motorsport's most legendary underdog victories and brings the rotary legacy into the collectable art-toy world.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Set for Rotary Anniversary

Mazda will release an official collaboration product with the manga series 'Initial D,' featuring 1/64-scale model cars of the Takahashi brothers' iconic RX-7s: the white FC3S Savanna RX-7 and yellow FD3S Anfini RX-7. The two-car set is designed to evoke a touge racing scene, with the cars offset at an angle against a dawn mountain-pass backdrop that forms a diorama when paired with the packaging, which also features author Shuichi Shigeno's signature and a rotary-themed quote from the manga. Sales begin on October 3, 2026, via the Mazda Online Shop and at Mazda Fan Festa 2026 at Fuji Speedway, with wider availability at Mazda dealerships across Japan from October 8.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda and rotary enthusiasts, this is the brand's first-ever official Initial D merchandise collaboration, marking the approaching 60th anniversary of Mazda's first production rotary-engined car and celebrating the RX-7 models that introduced countless fans worldwide to the joy of rotary-powered driving.

Source: [マツダ、ロータリーエンジン搭載車60周年を記念し、 漫画『頭文字D』との初の公式コラボレーションモデルカーを発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202610/261002a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda and ENEOS Team Up to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a collaboration to raise awareness and accelerate the adoption of low-carbon fuels. At the 37th Media Endurance Roadster Race, approximately 20 Roadster (MX-5) cars provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a bio-ethanol blended gasoline containing 20% ethanol. The event is scheduled for October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway, held within the MAZDA FAN FESTA framework.

**Why it matters:** This real-world motorsport trial of E20 bio-ethanol fuel in the iconic Roadster (MX-5) platform signals Mazda's continued commitment to carbon-neutral alternatives beyond electrification, a strategy that could influence future fuel choices for Mazda models worldwide.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Mark 787B Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has partnered with Medicom Toy to release a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B, celebrating the 35th anniversary of its historic 1991 Le Mans 24 Hours overall victory — the first by a Japanese car. The collectible bear-shaped figure features the 787B's signature livery and graphics, with a rotary engine emblem on the chest and a 35th-anniversary message on the back. It is available in two sizes — the palm-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm) and the larger 400% (approximately 28 cm) — both with poseable limbs, sold via the Mazda Online Shop.

**Why it matters:** The 787B's Le Mans triumph is one of motorsport's most celebrated moments, and this BE@RBRICK collaboration offers international Mazda enthusiasts a stylish, globally recognized collectible format to commemorate that legacy.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Two-Car Set

Mazda will release an official collaboration product with the manga 'Initial D,' featuring 1/64-scale model cars of the Takahashi brothers' iconic RX-7s: the white FC3S Savanna RX-7 and the yellow FD3S Anfini RX-7. The two-car set is packaged with a dawn mountain-pass backdrop and includes author Shuichi Shigeno's signature on the reverse, alongside a rotary-engine quote selected by Mazda employees from the manga. Sales begin October 3, 2026, via the Mazda Online Shop and at the Mazda Fan Festa event at Fuji Speedway, with wider availability at Mazda dealerships nationwide from October 8.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda enthusiasts, this is the first-ever official Mazda merchandise crossover with Initial D, celebrating the rotary engine's legacy through one of the most beloved automotive manga franchises ever produced.

Source: [マツダ、ロータリーエンジン搭載車60周年を記念し、 漫画『頭文字D』との初の公式コラボレーションモデルカーを発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202610/261002a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda and ENEOS Team Up to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel

Mazda and Japanese energy giant ENEOS have announced a partnership to raise awareness and accelerate adoption of low-carbon fuels. Around 20 Roadster (MX-5) cars competing in the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster Endurance Race will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE E20, a next-generation fuel blending gasoline with 20% bio-derived ethanol. The race is scheduled for October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway as part of the MAZDA FAN FESTA at FUJI SPEEDWAY event.

**Why it matters:** This real-world motorsport trial of E20 ethanol-blended fuel in Roadster (MX-5) race cars signals Mazda's active push toward carbon-neutral solutions beyond electrification, an approach that could influence how the brand develops and markets internal-combustion powertrains globally.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Team Up for 787B Le Mans 35th Anniversary Figure

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the MAZDA 787B racing car. The collectible bear figure features the 787B's iconic livery and graphics, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th anniversary message on the reverse. It is available in two sizes — the palm-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm tall) and the larger 400% (approximately 28 cm tall) — both with poseable limbs, sold through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP.

**Why it matters:** The 787B's 1991 Le Mans overall victory remains one of motorsport's most celebrated moments, and this limited collectible gives international Mazda fans a fresh, design-forward way to commemorate the 35th anniversary of that historic win.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

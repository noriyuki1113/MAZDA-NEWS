### Mazda and ENEOS Team Up to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a partnership aimed at raising awareness and accelerating adoption of low-carbon fuels. At the 37th Media Endurance Roadster Race — a long-running one-make event in which media professionals compete firsthand — approximately 20 Roadster (MX-5) vehicles provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a bio-ethanol blend containing 20% ethanol. The race is scheduled for October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway, held within the MAZDA FAN FESTA at FUJI SPEEDWAY event.

**Why it matters:** This collaboration signals Mazda's active investment in alternative low-carbon liquid fuels as part of its multi-pathway approach to carbon neutrality, with the iconic Roadster (MX-5) serving as a real-world testbed alongside other Japanese automakers.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Mark 787B's 35th Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has partnered with Medicom Toy for its first-ever BE@RBRICK collaboration, releasing a figure inspired by the MAZDA 787B that won the 1991 Le Mans 24 Hours — the first overall victory by a Japanese car. The 'BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B' figure is available through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP in two sizes: a palm-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm tall) and a larger 400% version (approximately 28 cm tall), both featuring the 787B's iconic livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th-anniversary message on the back. Mazda stated that the collaboration aims to bring the spirit of the 787B's achievement to both longtime fans and younger audiences who may be less familiar with the car's history.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda enthusiasts, this limited collectible is a rare officially licensed piece celebrating one of motorsport's most iconic moments — the rotary-powered 787B's historic Le Mans triumph — making it a highly desirable item for collectors worldwide.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Set for Rotary Anniversary

Mazda will release an official collaboration die-cast set with the manga 'Initial D,' featuring 1/64-scale models of the FC3S Savanna RX-7 and FD3S Anfini RX-7 driven by the Takahashi brothers in the series. The two-car set is packaged with a dawn mountain-pass backdrop and includes author Shuichi Shigeno's signature on the reverse alongside a rotary-themed quote chosen by Mazda staff. Sales begin on 3 October 2026 via the Mazda Online Shop and at Mazda Fan Festa 2026 at Fuji Speedway, with broader availability at Mazda dealerships nationwide from 8 October 2026.

**Why it matters:** For overseas Mazda enthusiasts, this marks the brand's first official Initial D merchandise collaboration and celebrates the approaching 60th anniversary of Mazda's first production rotary-engined car, making it a significant collector's piece tied to both automotive and pop-culture history.

Source: [マツダ、ロータリーエンジン搭載車60周年を記念し、 漫画『頭文字D』との初の公式コラボレーションモデルカーを発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202610/261002a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda and ENEOS Team Up to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a partnership to raise awareness of low-carbon fuels, centered on the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster (MX-5) endurance race. Approximately 20 Roadsters (MX-5s) provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a next-generation gasoline blended with 20% plant-derived bioethanol. The race is scheduled to take place on October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway as part of the MAZDA FAN FESTA at FUJI SPEEDWAY event.

**Why it matters:** This collaboration signals Mazda's active commitment to real-world testing of low-carbon fuel alternatives using the iconic Roadster (MX-5) as a demonstrator, reflecting the brand's broader carbon-neutrality strategy that could influence future fuel compatibility decisions globally.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Mark 787B Le Mans 35th Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B race car to mark the 35th anniversary of its historic 1991 Le Mans 24 Hours overall victory — the first by a Japanese manufacturer. The figure is available in two sizes, approximately 7 cm (100%) and 28 cm (400%), both featuring the 787B's signature livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th-anniversary message on the back. The figures are sold exclusively through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP.

**Why it matters:** The 787B's Le Mans victory is one of motorsport's most celebrated moments, and this limited collectible offers global Mazda fans a fresh, design-forward way to own a piece of that rotary racing legacy.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda and ENEOS Partner to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a collaboration to raise awareness and accelerate the adoption of low-carbon fuels. Around 20 Roadster (MX-5) vehicles participating in the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster Endurance Race will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a gasoline blend containing 20% bio-based ethanol. The race is scheduled for October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway, held within the MAZDA FAN FESTA at FUJI SPEEDWAY event.

**Why it matters:** This initiative signals Mazda's active commitment to real-world testing of alternative low-carbon fuels in a motorsport setting, which could influence the direction of the company's multi-solution approach to carbon neutrality for production vehicles globally.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Team Up for 787B Le Mans Anniversary Figure

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B race car. The collectible figure features the 787B's signature livery and graphics, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th anniversary message on the back. It is available in two sizes — the palm-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm) and the larger 400% (approximately 28 cm) — both with poseable limbs, sold through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP.

**Why it matters:** The 787B's 1991 Le Mans overall victory remains one of motorsport's most celebrated moments, and this limited collectible offers international Mazda fans a unique piece of memorabilia marking the 35th anniversary of that historic win.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

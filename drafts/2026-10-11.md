### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Honor 787B's 35th Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B race car. The collectible is available in two sizes — a palm-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm) and a larger 400% (approximately 28 cm) — both featuring the 787B's distinctive livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th-anniversary message on the back. The release marks 35 years since the 787B became the first Japanese car to claim overall victory at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1991.

**Why it matters:** For Mazda enthusiasts worldwide, this limited collaboration merges the legendary motorsport heritage of the 787B with the global BE@RBRICK collectible culture, making it a compelling piece of Mazda history in a fresh, accessible format.

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Set for Rotary Anniversary

Mazda will release an official collaboration with the manga series 'Initial D,' featuring a two-car 1/64-scale model set depicting the Takahashi brothers' iconic RX-7s: the white FC3S Savanna RX-7 and the yellow FD3S Anfini RX-7. **Why it matters:** For Mazda enthusiasts worldwide, this marks the brand's first-ever official Initial D merchandise and celebrates the approaching 60th anniversary of Mazda's first production rotary-engined car, underscoring the enduring cultural and engineering legacy of the rotary engine.

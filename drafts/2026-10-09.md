### Mazda and ENEOS Partner to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel at Roadster Race

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a collaboration to raise awareness of low-carbon fuels, centered on the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster (MX-5) endurance race scheduled for October 3–4, 2026. Approximately 20 Roadsters provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a next-generation fuel blending 20% bio-ethanol with gasoline to reduce CO2 emissions. The event takes place within the MAZDA FAN FESTA at Fuji Speedway, and will also feature a team from the cross-manufacturer carbon-neutral racing initiative known as the Kyōchō S-tai Waigaya Club.

**Why it matters:** This real-world racing trial of E20 bio-ethanol fuel signals Mazda's continued commitment to internal-combustion engine innovation as a viable path to carbon neutrality—a strategy directly relevant to fans who follow the brand's multi-solution approach to electrification.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Team Up to Celebrate 787B's 35th Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B race car. The collectible honors the 35th anniversary of the 787B's historic 1991 Le Mans 24 Hours victory — the first overall win at the race by a Japanese manufacturer. The figure is available in two sizes, approximately 7 cm (100%) and 28 cm (400%), both featuring the 787B's signature livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a anniversary message on the reverse.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda enthusiasts, this collaboration brings the legendary rotary-powered 787B's Le Mans legacy into a widely collected art-toy format, making it an accessible piece of motorsport history for fans of all ages.

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Set for Rotary Anniversary

Mazda will release an official collaboration product with the manga 'Initial D,' featuring 1/64-scale model cars of the Takahashi brothers' iconic RX-7s — the FC3S Savanna RX-7 and FD3S Anfini RX-7. The two-car set is packaged with a dawn mountain-pass diorama backdrop and includes creator Shuichi Shigeno's signature alongside a rotary-themed quote selected by Mazda staff from the manga. Sales begin on October 3, 2026, through the Mazda Online Shop and at Mazda Fan Festa 2026 at Fuji Speedway, with broader availability at Mazda dealerships nationwide from October 8.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda enthusiasts, this is the first official Mazda-branded Initial D merchandise and a meaningful tribute to the rotary engine's cultural legacy ahead of the 60th anniversary of the Cosmo Sport, Mazda's first production rotary-powered car.

Source: [マツダ、ロータリーエンジン搭載車60周年を記念し、 漫画『頭文字D』との初の公式コラボレーションモデルカーを発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202610/261002a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda to Showcase Co-Creation Tech at Japan Mobility Show BizWeek 2026

Mazda will participate in Japan Mobility Show BizWeek 2026, a business-focused event held at Makuhari Messe in Chiba Prefecture from October 13–16, joining automakers, startups, and research institutions to explore new collaborative opportunities. The company will take part in four programs: a startup matchmaking initiative called MeetUp-Box, a dedicated exhibition booth, a joint display, and a future mobility conference session featuring CEO Katsuhiro Moro. **Why it matters:** The event offers a rare public look at Mazda's active research partnerships and its practical steps toward realizing the human-centered AI and carbon-neutral technologies previewed in the MAZDA VISION X-COUPE and MAZDA VISION X-COMPACT concept vehicles.

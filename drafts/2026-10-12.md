### Mazda and ENEOS Partner to Promote Low-Carbon E20 Fuel at Roadster Race

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a collaboration to raise awareness of low-carbon fuels, centered on the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster (MX-5) endurance race scheduled for October 3–4, 2026. Approximately 20 Roadsters provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a next-generation gasoline blended with 20% plant-derived bioethanol that is expected to reduce CO2 emissions. The long-running media-participant race, held at Fuji Speedway as part of the MAZDA FAN FESTA event, will also see the cross-manufacturer 'Kyōchō S-Tai Waigaya Club' team—comprising Mazda, Toyota, Subaru, Nissan/NISMO, and Honda Racing—compete for a second consecutive year.

**Why it matters:** This collaboration signals Mazda's active, real-world testing of bioethanol-blended fuels as a viable carbon-neutral pathway for its internal-combustion engines, which is directly relevant to the brand's global multi-solution electrification strategy.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Celebrate 787B's 35th Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B racing car. The collectible figure features the 787B's distinctive livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th anniversary victory message on the reverse. It is available in two sizes — the standard 100% (approximately 7 cm) and the larger 400% (approximately 28 cm) — both featuring poseable limbs and sold through the Mazda Online Shop.

**Why it matters:** The 787B's 1991 Le Mans overall victory remains one of motorsport's most celebrated achievements, and this limited collectible offers international Mazda fans a fresh, design-forward way to commemorate the car's 35th anniversary.

### Mazda and Initial D team up for official RX-7 die-cast set

Mazda has announced its first-ever official collaboration with the manga series Initial D, producing a 1/64-scale two-car model set featuring the Takahashi brothers' iconic RX-7s. The set includes the white FC3S Savanna RX-7 and yellow FD3S Anfini RX-7, displayed at an angle to evoke the feel of a touge run, with packaging that depicts a mountain pass at dawn. The release is tied to the approaching 60th anniversary of Mazda's first production rotary-engined car, the Cosmo Sport, and will go on sale through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP and at Mazda Fan Festa 2026 at Fuji Speedway before reaching dealerships nationwide.

**Why it matters:** Initial D introduced a generation of fans worldwide to rotary-powered Mazdas, so this officially licensed collectible is a rare piece of merchandise that bridges the manga's cultural legacy with Mazda's rotary heritage milestone.

### Mazda to Join Japan Mobility Show BizWeek 2026 in October

Mazda will participate in Japan Mobility Show BizWeek 2026, a cross-industry business event held at Makuhari Messe in Chiba from October 13–16, organized by the Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association. The company will take part in four program formats: a startup matchmaking initiative called MeetUp-Box, a dedicated exhibition booth, a joint display, and a future mobility conference session featuring CEO Katsuhiro Moro. Highlights include a showcase of empathetic AI research developed with UK startup Thymia, a rotary engine concept unit demonstrating multi-fuel flexibility, and discussions on carbon-neutral fuels derived from microalgae.

**Why it matters:** This event offers a rare public window into Mazda's active R&D collaborations and its concrete steps toward carbon neutrality, including rotary engine innovation and AI-driven driving experiences previewed in the MAZDA VISION concepts shown in 2025.

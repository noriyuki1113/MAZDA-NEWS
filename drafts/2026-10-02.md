### Mazda and ENEOS to Run E20 Bioethanol Fuel at Roadster Endurance Race

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a partnership to promote low-carbon fuel awareness, centered on the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster Endurance Race. Approximately 20 Roadster (MX-5) race cars provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a gasoline blend containing 20% bioethanol derived from plant-based sources. The race is scheduled for October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway, held within the MAZDA FAN FESTA at FUJI SPEEDWAY event.

**Why it matters:** This collaboration signals Mazda's active commitment to real-world testing of low-carbon alternative fuels in a motorsport setting, reinforcing their multi-pathway approach to carbon neutrality that could eventually influence how Mazda-powered vehicles are fueled globally.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Team Up for 787B Le Mans Anniversary Figure

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B race car. The collectible figure features the 787B's distinctive livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th-anniversary victory message on the back, and is available in two sizes — the palm-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm) and the larger 400% (approximately 28 cm). Sales began through the Mazda Online Shop, marking the 35th anniversary of the 787B's historic 1991 Le Mans 24 Hours overall victory, the first by a Japanese manufacturer.

**Why it matters:** For global Mazda fans, this officially licensed collectible is a rare opportunity to own a piece of rotary and Le Mans history reimagined through one of the world's most recognisable designer toy formats.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda Wins Engineering Award for World-First Heat-Shielding Coating Technology

Mazda has received the 76th Society of Automotive Engineers of Japan Award (Technology Development Prize) for developing a world-first high-response thermal barrier coating for engine combustion chambers. The material, based on silicone resin combined with hollow inorganic particles and fine silica particles, retains heat during combustion to improve fuel economy and output, then cools rapidly afterward to prevent the intake-charge heating that typically saps torque. Mazda also developed advanced spray-painting simulation using cameras, laser visualization, and 3D computational fluid dynamics to apply the coating uniformly inside complex engine components.

**Why it matters:** This technology is already being validated in Mazda's MAZDA3 Bio concept racing entry in Japan's Super Taikyu Series, and Mazda has stated it aims to bring the innovation to future production vehicles.

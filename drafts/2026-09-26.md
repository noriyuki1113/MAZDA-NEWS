### Mazda Wins Engineering Award for World-First Heat-Shielding Engine Coating

Mazda has received the 76th Society of Automotive Engineers of Japan Award (Technology Development Prize) for developing a world-first high-response thermal barrier coating for engine combustion chambers. Unlike conventional ceramic-based heat shields, the new material — made from silicone resin combined with hollow inorganic particles and fine silica particles — retains heat during combustion to improve fuel economy and output, then dissipates heat rapidly afterward to prevent torque loss during intake. Mazda also used model-based development (MBD) and 3D computational fluid dynamics to simulate the entire coating and curing process, enabling uniform application inside complex engine components and faster optimization across different part geometries.

**Why it matters:** This award-winning thermal barrier technology is already being validated in Mazda's MAZDA3 Bio concept racing program in Japan's Super Taikyu Series, and Mazda has stated it is targeting future production vehicles — meaning it could eventually reach engines in cars sold worldwide.

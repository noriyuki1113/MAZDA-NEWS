### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Mark 787B Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B racing car. The release commemorates the 35th anniversary of the 787B's historic 1991 Le Mans 24 Hours overall victory — the first by a Japanese manufacturer. The collectible figure is available in two sizes, approximately 7 cm (100%) and 28 cm (400%), both featuring the 787B's signature livery, a rotary engine emblem on the chest, and a 35th-anniversary message on the back.

**Why it matters:** The 787B's Le Mans triumph is a cornerstone of Mazda's motorsport heritage celebrated by fans worldwide, and this limited collectible offers enthusiasts outside Japan a tangible way to commemorate that 35th anniversary milestone.

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Set for Rotary Anniversary

Mazda will release an official collaborative 1/64 scale model car two-pack featuring the FC3S Savanna RX-7 and FD3S Anfini RX-7 driven by the Takahashi brothers in the manga 'Initial D,' available from October 3, 2026 via the Mazda Online Shop and at Mazda Fan Festa at Fuji Speedway. The set commemorates the approaching 60th anniversary of Mazda's first mass-production rotary-engined car, the Cosmo Sport, and is packaged with a diorama-style mountain-pass background evoking scenes from the manga. **Why it matters:** For global Mazda and rotary enthusiasts, this marks the first official Mazda-branded collaboration with Initial D—one of the most influential car manga ever—making it a historically significant piece of rotary heritage merchandise ahead of the Cosmo Sport's 60th anniversary.

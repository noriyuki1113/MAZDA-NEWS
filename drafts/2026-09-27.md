### Mazda Wins Automotive Engineering Award for World-First Heat-Shielding Technology

Mazda has received the 76th Automotive Engineers' Award (Technology Development Prize) from the Society of Automotive Engineers of Japan, recognizing its development of a world-first high-response heat-shielding coating material for engine combustion chambers. Unlike conventional ceramic-based thermal barrier materials, Mazda's new coating — made from silicone resin combined with hollow inorganic particles and fine silica particles — retains heat during combustion but dissipates it quickly afterward, simultaneously improving fuel economy, power output, and torque. The technology has been undergoing real-world validation since 2023 aboard the MAZDA3 Bio concept (car No. 55) competing in the Super Taikyu Series ST-Q class, with the goal of applying it to future production vehicles.

**Why it matters:** This award-winning combustion chamber coating represents a potential leap forward in Mazda's Skyactiv engine efficiency philosophy, and its ongoing motorsport testing suggests it could eventually reach production cars sold worldwide.

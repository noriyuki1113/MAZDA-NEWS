### Mazda and ENEOS Partner to Promote E20 Bioethanol Fuel at Roadster Race

Mazda and Japanese energy company ENEOS have announced a collaboration to raise awareness and accelerate adoption of low-carbon fuels. At the 37th Media Taikyu Roadster Endurance Race, approximately 20 Roadster (MX-5) vehicles provided by Mazda will run on ENEOS EARTH ONE (E20), a gasoline blend containing 20% bioethanol derived from plant sources. The race is scheduled for October 3–4, 2026, at Fuji Speedway as part of the MAZDA FAN FESTA at FUJI SPEEDWAY event.

**Why it matters:** This partnership demonstrates Mazda's active commitment to carbon-neutral technologies through real-world motorsport use of next-generation biofuels, signaling the direction the company is heading beyond electrification alone.

### Mazda and BE@RBRICK Collaborate to Mark 787B's 35th Le Mans Anniversary

Mazda has launched its first-ever collaboration with Medicom Toy, producing a BE@RBRICK figure themed around the iconic MAZDA 787B race car. The collectible bears the 787B's signature livery and graphics, with a rotary engine emblem on the chest and a 35th anniversary message on the back. It is available in two sizes — the pocket-sized 100% (approximately 7 cm tall) and the larger 400% (approximately 28 cm tall) — both featuring poseable limbs and sold through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP.

**Why it matters:** The MAZDA 787B's 1991 Le Mans overall victory remains one of motorsport's most celebrated moments, and this limited collectible offers global fans a fresh, design-forward way to commemorate its 35th anniversary.

Source: [マツダとBE@RBRICKが初コラボレーション、 ル・マン優勝35周年記念「BE@RBRICK × MAZDA 787B」を発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202609/260924a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)

### Mazda Launches Official Initial D RX-7 Die-Cast Set for Rotary Anniversary

Mazda will release a 1/64 scale two-car model set featuring the FC3S Savanna RX-7 and FD3S Anfini RX-7 driven by the Takahashi brothers in the manga Initial D, marking the approaching 60th anniversary of Mazda's first mass-produced rotary-engined car, the Cosmo Sport. The set is being sold as official Mazda merchandise through the MAZDA ONLINE SHOP and select physical locations, with sales beginning October 3, 2026. The package design recreates a dawn mountain pass scene, and the rear panel features author Shuichi Shigeno's signature alongside a rotary-themed quote from the manga chosen by Mazda employees.

**Why it matters:** For Mazda enthusiasts worldwide, this is the first-ever official Mazda collaboration with Initial D — a manga that introduced a generation of fans to rotary-powered sports cars — making it a historically significant piece of Mazda memorabilia tied to the rotary engine's 60th anniversary.

Source: [マツダ、ロータリーエンジン搭載車60周年を記念し、 漫画『頭文字D』との初の公式コラボレーションモデルカーを発売](https://newsroom.mazda.com/ja/publicity/release/2026/202610/261002a.html) (Japanese only, as of this writing)
